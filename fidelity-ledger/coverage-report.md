## Remaining limits

The supplied collection does not contain the complete standalone Algeria and abolition reports previously claimed as directly mapped. Some colonial interpretation depends on the letters and the Companion; it is marked accordingly. Selected-letter OCR is substantially damaged and unsuitable for confident fine-grained style measurement. The Kergorlay collection dominates the measured correspondence family.

The audit's curation scores are editorial rubric judgments, not measured probabilities. Source units within one work are not independent historical witnesses, and duplicated letters across editions remain one item. Modern application is reasoned extrapolation after factual retrieval, not an attested historical verdict or a forecast validated by these tests.

No full books or extracted corpus are published. Exact editions, source spans, short evidence fragments, decisions, measurements, test items, and runtime hashes are available in this ledger.
