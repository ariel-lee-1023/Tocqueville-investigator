# Tocqueville — provenance

## 1. Weights and limits of scoring

The run uses projectibility 0.30, cost/refusal 0.25, expressive match 0.20, interactional visibility 0.15, and preoccupation 0.10. The thirty-nine candidate ratings in [scores.json](scores.json) are editor-assigned rubric judgments, not independent empirical probabilities. Equal ratings reflect rubric bands rather than a claimed precision of historical measurement. The six cut candidates are unsupported or overgeneralized old rules.

The principal material cost is the loss of useful departmental office after refusal of the post-coup oath. Administrative inconvenience, liberty beyond utility, and disagreement with a friend are different kinds of normative or relational divergence; the audit does not claim four measured personal losses.

## 3. Corpus and attribution

The five supplied files were inventoried and mechanically inspected. Eighteen broad slices supplied the coverage census; later-letter, Berryer, and two women-chapter subdivisions are targeted supplemental locators and do not add independent mass. Thirteen predominantly authorial units enter the style profiles. Mixed interviews, coauthored prison material, selected-letter editorial apparatus, and the Cambridge Companion do not. Duplicate Democracy and the Bonner Old Regime translation are excluded.

The supplied Delphi collection contains Reeve’s Old Regime translation with added posthumous chapters and another Bonner translation. This run uses the published-work portion and supplementary Languedoc discussion of the Reeve text. Its unusual chapter numbering differs from other editions; filename and line span are therefore part of each locator. The collection’s editorial synopsis incorrectly calls the first publication 1859 while also giving 1856. The synopsis is excluded as authorial evidence.

The letters include modern translation and severe OCR. The Kergorlay material in the older collected translation supplies the correspondence style baseline; selected modern letters supply particular judgments after passage inspection. The modern Companion supplies interpretive challenges, especially on empire and the scope of comparison; it is not a source of Tocqueville’s own phrasing. Complete standalone Algeria and abolition reports were not found, so the old package’s claim to have directly mapped them is withdrawn.

## 5. Registers and names

The accepted discovery proposes public analysis, private recollection, and correspondence. [registers.json](registers.json) contains the full distance matrix, forced splits, family assignments, and gradient. The default is public analysis. The corpus was not pooled into a single runtime baseline. Translation and genre are confounded, so the differences cannot be attributed to authorial choice alone.

The authorial name audit finds no literal “soft despotism” or “point of departure” in these selected English units. The former is explicitly a conventional label; the latter is an English rendering associated with the origin inquiry, not proof of a literal coined phrase in this translation. “Louis-Napoleon” is a modern hyphenated spelling; “Louis Napoleon” has literal authorial-text hits. Gobineau is securely the addressee of the inspected letters but has only one literal hit in the selected authorial supplement. Headings and addressee metadata do not become prose evidence. Substring occurrence counts, particularly “press,” are not concept counts. No claim that Tocqueville coined individualism is retained.

## 6. Tests and sampling

The corrected seed-42 split masks fifteen of 117 chapter units across six Democracy domains. Two body-exposed items were excluded, leaving thirteen scored. Ten receive stance-and-mechanism credit and three directional credit: 23/26 = 0.8846; hit_2 = 0.7692, hit_1 = 0.2308. Chapter headings remained visible, the same agent performed development and scoring, and model familiarity was not controlled. This is a limited reconstruction exercise, not independent held-out accuracy. It supplies no direct prediction score for Old Regime, Recollections, empire, or modern cases.

The final review rechecked those same predictions against the completed package and retained the partial-credit limitations in administration/spending, intermediate social rank, and etiquette abroad. It is a consistency review, not a second independent sample. Four inventoried divergences remain in the core; no unlogged omission remains.

The corrected name-masked source discrimination scored 12/12 with seed 71. Its source separation is informative, but familiar material, genre, translation, and residual dates can contribute. The initial seed-42 result was superseded after footnote and metadata contamination was noticed.

The three generated samples were measured with the same style script. Initial means were 25.32, 19.15, and 15.44 words for public analysis, recollection, and correspondence; voice guidance and samples were revised. Final means are 31.81, 30.44, and 20.75, against source means 35.49, 29.62, and 20.67. The public sample has 509 words and addresses a contested proposal. Avoid-list violations are zero. Pronoun and punctuation modulation remains incomplete, and the revised samples are development material. Style is therefore partial, not an unconditional pass.

Mechanical package checks and source replay are reported separately. [fidelity.json](fidelity.json) binds the current results to the canonical runtime content hash; [test-hashes.json](test-hashes.json) records per-result association and sample hashes.

## 7. Comparison with the earlier package

The old text prescribed a single master cause, origins as unfolding seeds, mores as a residual explanation, and comparison as one-factor isolation. It imposed a single first-person survey voice, declared convictions fixed, claimed an 1859 factual freeze while also asking for live retrieval, and reported scores without published test items. The new rules explicitly address those failure modes. No numerical baseline uplift is claimed because the old reported percentages are not reproducible.
