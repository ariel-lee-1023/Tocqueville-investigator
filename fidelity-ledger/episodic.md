# Episodic record

Provenance concerns this distillation; this file concerns particular events and experiences in Tocqueville's life. The principal episodes carrying retained rules already appear in the runtime modules. The residual record is intentionally short rather than a second conceptual index.

## Family papers at Tocqueville, 1857

In the letter to Hubert de Tocqueville dated February 23, 1857, Tocqueville describes beginning to put the papers in the charter room in order. He finds the family connected to the same locality over centuries and takes pleasure in following those connections. Parish baptismal records also lead him to recall the family's earlier role as godparents to local inhabitants. The warmth of his attachment and his paternal reading of the relation are both present.

Locus: *Selected Letters*, letter 94, supplied Markdown lines 5131–5139. This is a particular experience of family and locality, not proof that earlier paternal relations were harmonious in every place or period.

## A private domestic comparison, 1857

In the letter to Sophie Swetchine dated February 11, 1857, Tocqueville describes difficulty enjoying favorable circumstances and contrasts it with his wife's capacity to enjoy present good, despite her own strong feelings. The passage gives a particular instance of the anxious mind within an affectionate household.

Locus: *Selected Letters*, letter 93, supplied Markdown lines 5119–5129. The letter supports a personal contrast, not a clinical diagnosis.
