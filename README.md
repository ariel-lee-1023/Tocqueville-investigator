# Let Alexis de Tocqueville serve as your investigator

I am Alexis de Tocqueville (29 July 1805). You tell me that a central office
could conduct the business of several neighborhoods with greater regularity.
I can readily believe it. I should still wish to know what the inhabitants
will cease to do together when the office takes charge, and where they will
learn the judgment and perseverance which their common affairs formerly required.

The administrative result and the effect upon the citizen may differ. Yet I
would not conclude that every local power serves liberty: a neighborhood can
defend a privilege as readily as a right. We must examine who decides, who
pays, who is excluded, and who can correct a mistake. Comparison becomes
useful when it tests one of these relations.

Bring me a reform whose consequences seem obvious to both its friends and its
enemies. I shall look for the practice each has omitted. Equality changes men's
expectations and their relations with one another; it does not decide in advance
whether they will become capable of governing themselves. The choices that
form their habits remain part of the inquiry.

This investigator examines democracy, institutions, social habits, and liberty.
It retains Tocqueville's tensions, including his defense of liberty at home
alongside French colonial ambition. Contemporary cases apply his methods;
they do not supply historical endorsements or independently validated forecasts.


Follow a formal reform through the practices it changes, the habits citizens learn, and the remaining opportunities to act together.

## What to bring

**Suggested first prompt:**

> A city wants to replace neighborhood budgets with a central office. Here are
> the current and proposed arrangements. Investigate who will decide, pay,
> execute, and answer for mistakes, and what residents will gain or lose the
> opportunity to do together.

Bring actual practices as well as the legal plan. The inquiry distinguishes
national coordination from administrative tutelage and asks how institutions
train habits. Greater regularity may have a civic cost; a local arrangement
may also perpetuate exclusion. Neither consequence follows from the label alone.

Other questions shift the object of inquiry:

- **Prosperity accompanied by greater discontent:** Bring what has improved, the
  expectations it opens and the barriers that remain. Investigate why a diminished
  burden may become less tolerable without treating the outcome as inevitable.
- **A reform borrowed from another country:** Compare the associations, social
  relations and administrative practices that sustain it. An effect in America
  is not automatically a recipe for France or a present-day institution.
- **A letter disagreeing with a friend:** Bring the claim, the relationship and
  what must be said plainly. Examine how affection and intellectual refusal can
  coexist without inventing a private historical exchange.
- **Liberty at home alongside colonial ambition:** Read the judgments on slavery,
  race and French Algeria in their circumstances. Preserve the tension rather
  than turning the record into a consistent defense of equal freedom.

These are intended uses, not validated forecasts. Public analysis is the default;
a requested political recollection gives more space to actors and conduct, while
correspondence permits affection, disagreement and unfinished thought. No form licenses
invented memories of modern events.

## Getting started

```sh
git clone https://github.com/ariel-lee-1023/tocqueville-investigator.git tocqueville-investigator
```

Open the folder with an agent that reads [AGENTS.md](AGENTS.md), which activates
the local perspective while preserving requests to change modes or maintain files.
For another skill host, install the root under `tocqueville-investigator-perspective`;
this skill name differs from the repository name.
Invoke `tocqueville-investigator-perspective` through your host's skill selector, or ask it to use this perspective with one of the situations above.

Responses default to **English**, the language of the distilled editions and voice
calibration. Explicitly request another language, for example “Answer in Chinese for
this conversation,” to change it for that scope. A message in another language alone
does not change the default.

## Sources and contributions

Tocqueville's personal record runs from the 1820s to 1859. The five supplied volumes
combine his writings with edited collections and later scholarship. Alternative
translations and duplicate works do not provide independent corroboration.

| --- | --- | --- |
| How do equal conditions affect the capacity for liberty? | Alexis de Tocqueville, *Democracy in America* (1835/1840), also represented in the collected works | Associations, public opinion, belief, work and everyday relations. [Democratic society](references/clusters/democratic-society.md) |
| What persists beneath an apparent revolution? | Tocqueville, *The Old Regime*, in the collected works | Administrative continuity, class separation and revolutionary preparation. [Old regime](references/clusters/old-regime.md) |
| What can an investigator learn from another person's account? | *Journey to America*; travel and inquiry material in the collections | Comparison and field inquiry, keeping interviewees' statements distinct from Tocqueville's. [Inquiry](references/clusters/inquiry-and-correspondence.md) |
| How can disagreement survive inside a friendship? | *Selected Letters on Politics and Society* and collected correspondence | Personal address, doubt, affection and the limits of inference from particular addressees. [Correspondence](references/clusters/inquiry-and-correspondence.md) |

## Scope and current status

*This introduction is a newly written, source-grounded persona reconstruction,
not a historical quotation. [Editorial and birth-date notes](fidelity-ledger/provenance.md#readme-editorial-review-2026-10-30).*

**Historical diagnostics only; no independent modern forecasting or French
stylometric fidelity has been established.** The source-sample recognition check
identified the sampled materials. A heading-visible reconstruction check was scored
by the same agent, so it was not an independent prediction benchmark. Generated samples
still differed in pronoun and punctuation patterns. Later loading changes have not
been reassessed; historical results retain their original inputs and scope.

The [coverage report](fidelity-ledger/coverage-report.md) separates primary prose,
interviewees' answers, mixed documents, editorial material and scholarship. OCR-damaged
selected letters were excluded from the voice baseline. Later publication does not
extend Tocqueville's lifetime knowledge. His judgments about slavery, race, women and
colonial rule retain tensions that a new application must not smooth away.

The repository's `python3 scripts/validate.py` check concerns structure and links,
not literary likeness. [Provenance](fidelity-ledger/provenance.md) and the
[reproduction notes](fidelity-ledger/reproduction.md) distinguish source review,
measurements and diagnostic results. The current revision adds an explicit English default and changes runtime inputs.
Historical assessments do not certify these changes.

**The revised runtime is a Candidate without a new recognition assessment.** This
maintenance preserves the existing material while aligning persona prose, host rules
and the placement of the introduction notice. See the [maintenance record](fidelity-ledger/provenance.md#skill-and-readme-alignment-2026-10-31)
for the checked scope and any pre-existing package issues. No new tests were added.
